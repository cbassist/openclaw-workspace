## 1. Mission & Positioning

**Core Mission:**
To develop advanced biomechanical systems that restore and enhance human mobility through precision engineering, materials science, and human-centered design.

**High-Level Positioning:**
1215 Labs LLC operates at the intersection of:

* orthopedic implant innovation
* prosthetic system design
* human augmentation research

The company focuses on **early-stage R&D, prototyping, and translational engineering**, not mass manufacturing or direct-to-consumer sales.

## 2. Core Domains of Work

The company operates in three primary domains:

### 2.1 Orthopedic Implants

* Hip replacement systems
* Knee replacement systems
* Focus areas:

  * improved wear surfaces
  * implant longevity
  * biomechanical alignment
  * materials optimization

---

### 2.2 Prosthetic Systems

* Advanced prosthetic limb components
* Emphasis on:

  * mechanical performance
  * comfort and ergonomics
  * modularity
  * integration with emerging control systems

---

### 2.3 Human Augmentation / Exoskeleton Research

* Early-stage exoskeleton concepts
* Mobility assistance systems
* Load-bearing augmentation
